# Privacy Policy for DevAlarm

**Effective Date: August 5, 2026**

Your privacy is extremely important to us. DevAlarm (the "Application") is built as a local, privacy-focused utility app. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**DevAlarm does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.**

*   **No Personal Data:** The Application does not require any account registration, email addresses, usernames, or contact information.
*   **Fully Local Operation:** All alarm schedules, configuration preferences, and completed programming puzzles are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage. This data never leaves your device.
*   **Audio and Notifications:** The Application schedules local notifications and synthesizes alarm tones natively using your device's operating system utilities (`UserNotifications` and `AVAudioEngine`). No background telemetry or audio recordings are processed or uploaded.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

The Application does not include third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, tracking tools, or external analytics frameworks. No cookies or tracking tags are utilized.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any personal information, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards for users of all ages.

---

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
